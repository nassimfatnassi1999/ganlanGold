# Launch readiness

Technical completion does not authorize publication. Every blocking item below needs an accountable owner and recorded evidence.

## Blocking business/legal approvals

- [ ] Legal operating entity name, registration/tax details where required, registered address, representative, formal email, jurisdiction, and legal-page wording approved.
- [ ] Privacy controller/contact, lawful bases, retention periods, deletion/correction process, processors, international-transfer position, cookie position, and incident obligations approved by counsel.
- [ ] Final USD prices, quote validity, MOQ wording, Incoterm guidance, exclusions, document list, and contact values approved by commercial leadership.
- [ ] Approved Chinese brand name and professional Chinese legal/regulatory copy supplied if required; no transliteration has been invented.
- [ ] Product classification, labels, ingredient/composition, origin, quality statements, certifications, import/export licences, and China-market compliance reviewed by qualified specialists.

## Blocking asset approvals

- [ ] Usage rights and final approval recorded for all four product photos, hero composition, grove/facility imagery, wordmark, and decorative art.
- [ ] Approved high-resolution production photos and final label artwork replace provisional V7 references where required.
- [ ] Lassad and Fouad QR images scan to the intended identities on representative WeChat devices; screenshots/date/tester recorded.
- [ ] Product labels and QR codes verified at 320, 375, 390, 430, 768, 1024, and 1440 px on representative devices.

## Infrastructure and operations

- [ ] Approved domain, DNS, VPS/region, monitoring, log storage, alert routing, SMTP provider, sender authentication, and backup destination configured.
- [ ] Independent production secrets generated and stored in an approved secret manager; no `.env` or dumps committed.
- [ ] TLS issuance/renewal, health monitoring, database disk alerts, ECB/SMTP reachability, and target-China reachability tested.
- [ ] Backup completed, encrypted off-host, and restored successfully to an isolated environment.
- [ ] Deployment and rollback rehearsal completed with named operators.
- [ ] First administrator created with an individual account; credential recovery/offboarding procedure approved.

## Mainland China decision

- [ ] Hosting jurisdiction decided.
- [ ] If mainland hosting/CDN is used, domain/real-name requirements, ICP filing/licence, public-security registration, provider eligibility, cross-border data, and content obligations confirmed externally.
- [ ] No filing, licence, registration, regulatory approval, or performance guarantee is claimed before evidence exists.

## Release evidence

- [ ] Full typecheck, lint, database-backed unit/integration tests, production build, and desktop/mobile Playwright suite pass on the release revision.
- [ ] Public and authenticated browser runtime checked with no console, hydration, network, or CSP error.
- [ ] Keyboard, focus, labels/errors, reduced-motion behavior, overflow, and WCAG A/AA automated audit checked.
- [ ] Metadata, canonical, OG, JSON-LD, robots, sitemap, health response, and production security headers inspected.
- [ ] Specification audit and acceptance checklist show release evidence and explicitly retain external blockers.

Launch authorization should record release revision, date/time, approver, deployment operator, backup identifier, rollback point, and unresolved accepted risks.
