# GANLAN GOLD Brand Guidelines

Status: implementation guidance derived from V7; not a legal or regulatory approval document.

## Positioning

GANLAN GOLD is presented as a premium Moroccan olive-oil partner for Chinese importers, distributors, and professional buyers. Communication is commercially precise, quietly confident, natural, and hospitable.

## Voice

- **Credible, not grandiose:** describe coordination, provenance, and commercial steps without unsupported superlatives.
- **Warm, not casual:** welcome visits and inquiries while avoiding consumer-lifestyle hype.
- **Precise, not legalistic:** qualify Incoterms, exclusions, rate dates, and document availability in plain language.
- **Bilingual by hierarchy:** Simplified Chinese leads; concise English follows at smaller visual weight.

Use a locally available Chinese serif stack for display text and a Chinese-capable system sans stack for controls/body text. Do not request fonts from Google-hosted endpoints. Photography favors natural light, olive greens, warm stone, real packaging proportions, and legible labels. Original/reference images must never be stretched, heavily cropped across label content, or used to carry mutable prices and conditions.

## Organic editorial anchor

- Sand-toned light surfaces, deep-green dark fields, 20–28 px rounded editorial image frames, and 1–2% grain.
- Spacious 4/8-point rhythm; high-contrast typography; tabular figures for prices and quantities.
- A thin gold traceability line connects sourcing, products, preparation, and handover.
- Motion is gentle, optional, and restricted to transform/opacity; reduced-motion users see the final state immediately.

## Brand-name rule

`GANLAN GOLD` is the approved prominent international name. No Chinese brand name is presented as final. Chinese product wording and label artwork remain subject to qualified review before launch/printing.

## Asset approval rule

The V7 product images and derived hero composition are provisional design assets. The V7 Lassad/Fouad QR images are the specified official assignments but still require target-device scan verification. Root marketing images have no supplied provenance/licence metadata and are treated as provisional.
